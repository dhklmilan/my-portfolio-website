Ecosystem Response to Burn Severity

Terai Arc Landscape (TAL), Nepal

Burn Severity Methodology

Burn severity was assessed using pre- and post-fire Normalized Burn Ratio (NBR) derived from NIR and SWIR bands. The difference between pre- and post-fire NBR (dNBR) was calculated and classified into burn-severity classes based on the USGS burn severity classification.

Total burned area was calculated by summing the areas classified as High, Moderate-high, Moderate-low, and Low Severity.
